Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

New Music Pulse is a personal tool run by its owner to maintain a YouTube playlist of new music videos. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

  • Public YouTube information (video titles, channels, publish dates, view counts) through the YouTube Data API.
  • The owner's own New Music Pulse YouTube channel, only to create and update its playlist. Access is granted by the owner through Google sign-in with the YouTube scope.

What it stores

A list of the videos on the playlist and weekly notes about which videos were found, kept privately by the owner. No personal information about viewers or other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
